# Small talk

Small talk tốt nhất khi ngắn và liên quan đến tình huống hiện tại. Dùng công thức: mở câu → phản hồi chi tiết → chia sẻ một ý → chuyển chủ đề hoặc kết thúc.

## Quick phrases

| Moment | Natural sentence | Simpler alternative | More professional alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | How is your morning going? | Good morning so far? | How has your morning been so far? |
| Busy day | Is it a busy day for you? | Busy today? | Do you have a full schedule today? |
| Weekend | Did you have a good weekend? | Good weekend? | Did you get a chance to relax over the weekend? |
| Weather | The rain was heavy this morning. | A lot of rain today. | The weather made the journey in quite slow today. |
| Office | Is this your first time in this office? | First time here? | Have you worked from this office before? |
| Event | What did you think of the session? | Good session? | Was there anything from the session that stood out to you? |
| Fast speech | Sorry, I missed the last part. | Could you repeat that? | Could you say that once more, a little more slowly? |
| Transition | By the way, are you joining the next meeting? | Next meeting? | By the way, will you be joining the next session? |
| Close | Good talking with you. See you later. | See you later. | It was good catching up. I will let you get back to your day. |

## Realistic dialogue

### Before a meeting

**You:** Hi Claire, how is your week going?  
**Claire:** Busy, but good. We have a release on Friday.  
**You:** I know that feeling. We also have two reviews this week.  
**Claire:** Good luck with those.  
**You:** Thanks. Is Friday’s release a major one?  
**Claire:** Mostly fixes, but one change is quite sensitive.  
**You:** I hope it goes smoothly. Shall we get ready for the meeting?  

### Meeting a Singapore colleague

**You:** Hi Daniel, good to meet you in person. How was the flight?  
**Daniel:** Short and easy, thanks.  
**You:** Is this your first visit to our office?  
**Daniel:** Yes. The area has changed a lot since my last trip to Vietnam.  
**You:** It changes quickly. If you need lunch or transport suggestions, let me know.  
**Daniel:** Thanks, I appreciate that.  

### Corridor conversation with a senior manager

**Manager:** How are things going?  
**You:** Good overall. I am reviewing two projects this week.  
**Manager:** Any major concerns?  
**You:** One deployment risk needs attention, but the team has a clear action plan.  
**Manager:** Good to hear.  
**You:** I will keep you posted if the risk changes. Enjoy the rest of your day.  

### Joining a group conversation

**Colleague:** We were just talking about weekend plans.  
**You:** Nice. Is anyone going somewhere interesting?  
**Colleague:** Marie is going to Da Lat.  
**You:** Great choice. It should be cooler there. Have you been before, Marie?  
**Marie:** No, it is my first time.  
**You:** Then the coffee farms may be worth a visit.  

## Conversation repair

- Sorry, I did not catch the name of the place.
- I heard the first part, but I missed the end.
- When you say “next week”, do you mean Monday or later in the week?
- Let me check that I understood you correctly.

## Useful collocations

- **a full schedule**, **a quiet weekend**, **heavy traffic**, **catch up**
- **stand out**, **go smoothly**, **keep someone posted**, **join a conversation**
- **get back to your day**, **good to meet you in person**, **first time here**
