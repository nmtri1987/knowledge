# Dinner and drinks

Dùng cho tối thứ Năm, sau workshop hoặc khi khách HQ sang Việt Nam. Lời mời nên thoải mái và luôn cho người khác cách từ chối.

## Quick phrases

| Situation | Natural sentence | Simpler alternative | More professional alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invite | Are you joining us tonight? | Coming tonight? | We are having dinner after work. You are very welcome to join. |
| Time | Shall we meet around seven? | Seven o’clock? | Would seven o’clock work for everyone? |
| Place | It is a quiet place near the river. | It is near the river. | I picked somewhere quiet so we can talk comfortably. |
| Drink | Would you like beer, wine, or something without alcohol? | What would you like? | What can I get you to drink? |
| Toast | Cheers! It is good to have everyone here. | Cheers! | Cheers, and thank you for spending the evening with us. |
| Decline | I will stay with sparkling water tonight. | Water for me. | I am not drinking tonight, but I am happy to join dinner. |
| Limit | I will have just one tonight. | Just one for me. | I will keep it light tonight. |
| Leave | I will head home after this one. | I am leaving soon. | I will call it a night after this drink. |
| Transport | How are you getting home? | Taxi home? | Have you arranged transport back? |
| Close | Thanks for tonight. I had a good time. | Thanks. Good night. | Thank you for a very enjoyable evening. |

## Realistic dialogue

### Thursday drinks with colleagues

**Colleague:** Are we still going out tonight?  
**You:** Yes, around seven. Would you like to join?  
**Colleague:** Sure. Is it formal?  
**You:** No, very casual. We will have food and one or two drinks.  
**Colleague:** Great. I may leave early.  
**You:** Of course. Come for as long as you like.  
**Colleague:** Where are you going?  
**You:** A quiet place near the river. I will send you the location.  

### With a visiting HQ manager

**You:** How has your week in Vietnam been so far?  
**Manager:** Busy, but very interesting.  
**You:** Have you had any time to explore the city?  
**Manager:** Only a short walk near the hotel.  
**You:** If you have time tomorrow, the river area is pleasant in the evening.  
**Manager:** Good idea. Is it far from here?  
**You:** About fifteen minutes by taxi, depending on traffic.  

### Leaving without making it awkward

**Manager:** Would you like another drink?  
**You:** Thanks, but I will stop here. I have an early start tomorrow.  
**Manager:** No problem.  
**You:** I had a good time. How are you getting home?  
**Manager:** I will book a car.  
**You:** Great. Have a safe trip home. See you tomorrow.  

## Safe conversation topics

- Food, travel, weekend plans, sports, books and city recommendations.
- Ask about experience: “What did you enjoy most?”
- Avoid pushing for personal details, alcohol or political opinions.

## Useful vocabulary and collocations

- **a casual dinner** — bữa tối thoải mái
- **go out for a drink** — đi uống cùng nhau
- **keep it light** — uống/ăn vừa phải
- **something without alcohol** — đồ uống không cồn
- **make a toast** — nâng ly chúc mừng
- **call it a night** — kết thúc buổi tối
- **an early start** — phải bắt đầu sớm hôm sau
- **get home safely** — về nhà an toàn
