# Travel conversations

Travel là chủ đề dễ dùng với đồng nghiệp Pháp và Singapore. Hỏi một câu, phản hồi vào chi tiết vừa nghe rồi chia sẻ ngắn về mình.

## Quick phrases

| Situation | Natural sentence | Simpler alternative | More professional alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recent trip | How was your trip? | Good trip? | How did the trip go overall? |
| Business trip | Was the schedule very busy? | Busy schedule? | Did you have much time between meetings? |
| Summer | Are you planning any trips this summer? | Any summer travel? | Do you have plans for the summer break? |
| Family | Are you travelling with the family? | Family trip? | Will the whole family be travelling together? |
| Duration | How long are you staying? | How many days? | How long will you be there this time? |
| Recommend | If you like quiet places, try Ninh Binh. | Try Ninh Binh. | If you prefer somewhere quieter, Ninh Binh is worth considering. |
| Transport | It is easier to get around by metro. | Take the metro. | The metro is probably the easiest way to get around. |
| Weather | It can be very humid in the afternoon. | Afternoons are humid. | I would plan outdoor activities for the morning. |
| Return | When do you get back? | Back when? | When will you be back in the office? |
| Wish | Have a great trip. | Enjoy your trip. | I hope you and your family have a wonderful trip. |

## Realistic dialogue

### Manager’s summer holiday

**You:** Do you have any travel plans this summer?  
**Manager:** We may spend three weeks in Japan.  
**You:** That sounds lovely. Will you visit family as well?  
**Manager:** Yes, for a few days, then we will travel around.  
**You:** Which area are you most looking forward to?  
**Manager:** Probably Hokkaido. It should be cooler there.  
**You:** Good choice. The summer here will be very hot.  

### Colleague returning from France

**You:** Welcome back. How was the trip?  
**Colleague:** Good, but the flight was long.  
**You:** Did you manage to sleep on the plane?  
**Colleague:** Only for two hours.  
**You:** That is tough. I hope your afternoon is not too busy.  
**Colleague:** Luckily, I only have one meeting.  

### Helping a visitor plan a weekend

**Visitor:** I have Saturday free. Where should I go?  
**You:** Do you prefer the city, the beach or somewhere quiet?  
**Visitor:** Somewhere quiet, with good scenery.  
**You:** I would suggest Ninh Binh. It is better for an overnight trip.  
**Visitor:** Is it easy to get there?  
**You:** Yes. A private car is the simplest option if your time is limited.  

## Follow-up questions

- What was the best part of the trip?
- Was anything different from what you expected?
- Would you go back?
- Which place would you recommend for a first visit?
- Did you find any good local food?

## Useful collocations

- **summer break**, **business trip**, **direct flight**, **connecting flight**
- **travel light**, **pack for the weather**, **get around**, **avoid rush hour**
- **worth a visit**, **less crowded**, **an overnight trip**, **a first-time visitor**
- **look forward to**, **get back**, **recover from jet lag**
