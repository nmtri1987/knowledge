# Lunch conversations

Mục tiêu: bắt đầu tự nhiên, chia sẻ một chi tiết của mình, hỏi tiếp một câu và kết thúc nhẹ nhàng. Dùng câu ngắn để vẫn rõ khi nhà hàng ồn.

## Quick phrases

| Situation | Natural sentence | Simpler alternative | More professional alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invite | Shall we get lunch? | Lunch? | Would you like to join us for lunch? |
| Choose | What do you feel like eating? | What would you like? | Do you have a preference today? |
| Recommend | The grilled fish is good and not too spicy. | The fish is good. | I would recommend the grilled fish. It is quite mild. |
| Check diet | Is there anything you do not eat? | Any food you avoid? | Do you have any dietary preferences? |
| Share | I usually come here when I want something light. | I come here sometimes. | This is one of my usual places for a light lunch. |
| Ask back | What do you normally have for lunch in France? | What do you eat in France? | What would a typical lunch be for you in France? |
| Work bridge | After lunch, I would like your view on one point. | Can I ask you something later? | When we get back, could I get your view on one technical point? |
| Pay | Shall we split the bill? | Split the bill? | Would you prefer to split the bill? |
| Time | We should probably head back in ten minutes. | Back in ten minutes? | We have about ten minutes before the next meeting. |
| Close | That was nice. Thanks for joining. | Thanks. That was good. | It was good catching up over lunch. |

## Realistic dialogue

### Choosing a local dish

**You:** Shall we try the Vietnamese place today?  
**Manager:** Yes. What do you recommend?  
**You:** The grilled fish is good and not too spicy.  
**Manager:** That sounds good. What comes with it?  
**You:** Rice, vegetables and a light sauce. Do you eat much spicy food?  
**Manager:** A little, but not too much.  
**You:** Then this should be fine. I will order the same.  

### Talking about home cooking

**Manager:** Do you normally cook at home?  
**You:** A few times a week. I usually make something simple.  
**Manager:** What is your easiest dish?  
**You:** Fried rice with vegetables. It is quick and hard to get wrong. What about you?  
**Manager:** My wife usually cooks Japanese food.  
**You:** Nice. Which dish do your children ask for when they come home?  
**Manager:** Usually curry rice.  
**You:** That is a good family meal.  

### Moving gently back to work

**You:** How is the food?  
**Manager:** Very good. I like the sauce.  
**You:** Me too. By the way, when we get back, could I get your view on the rollout plan?  
**Manager:** Of course. What is the concern?  
**You:** The rollback step is still unclear. I will show you the two options after lunch.  
**Manager:** Good. Let us take ten minutes for it.  
**You:** Perfect. No rush now. Let us finish lunch first.  

## When you do not hear clearly

- Sorry, I missed the last part. What did you say about the dish?
- Could you say the name again, a little more slowly?
- Did you say **rice** or **fries**?
- I know the place, but I have not tried that dish.

## Useful vocabulary and collocations

- **grab lunch / get lunch** — đi ăn trưa
- **something light** — món nhẹ bụng
- **a filling dish** — món ăn no
- **not too spicy** — không quá cay
- **a local specialty** — đặc sản địa phương
- **dietary preference** — lựa chọn hoặc hạn chế ăn uống
- **split the bill** — chia hóa đơn
- **head back** — quay lại văn phòng
- **catch up over lunch** — trò chuyện cập nhật trong bữa trưa
- **What comes with it?** — món này ăn kèm gì?
