# Food, Travel & Small-Talk Playbook
## Practical English for Vietnamese–French workplace conversations

> Mục tiêu: dùng khi ăn trưa, dinner, uống bia, đi công tác, đón đồng nghiệp/sếp nước ngoài, hoặc small talk trước/sau meeting. Ưu tiên câu ngắn, dễ nói, dễ mở rộng thành hội thoại qua lại.

## 1. Công thức nói về bất kỳ món ăn nào

Dùng 5 bước: **What is it? → What is inside? → How do you eat it? → What does it taste like? → Is there something similar in your country?**

> This is called **[dish]**.  
> It’s made with **[ingredients]**.  
> You usually eat it with **[side dish / sauce]**.  
> It tastes **[crispy / fresh / rich / spicy / mild]**.  
> Do you have anything similar in France?

Từ vựng mô tả: crispy, crunchy, soft, tender, chewy, juicy, rich, light, fresh, mild, spicy, sweet, sour, salty, bitter, savory, smoky, creamy, refreshing, filling.

## 2. Bánh xèo

Từ vựng: Vietnamese savory pancake, fish sauce, dipping sauce, sweet-and-sour fish sauce, herbs, fresh herbs, lettuce, bean sprouts, shrimp/prawns, pork, beef, egg, mushrooms, vegetarian, crispy, wrap, dip.

Câu dễ nói:
- This is bánh xèo.
- It’s crispy on the outside.
- You eat it with fresh herbs and lettuce.
- You wrap a small piece with vegetables.
- Then you dip it in the fish sauce.
- There are many different kinds of herbs.
- The vegetables make it feel lighter.
- The herbs help balance the rich flavor.
- This one has shrimp and pork.
- You can also have beef, egg, seafood, or a vegetarian version.
- The vegetarian one usually has mushrooms and vegetables.
- After the meal, we often drink iced tea.

“Đỡ ngán”:
- It feels lighter with the herbs.
- The vegetables make it less heavy.
- The herbs balance the richness.
- It’s easier to eat with fresh vegetables.

Hội thoại:

**You:** Have you tried bánh xèo before?  
**Manager:** No, first time.  
**You:** It’s crispy, and you eat it with fresh herbs and lettuce.  
**Manager:** How do I eat it?  
**You:** Take a small piece, wrap it with vegetables, then dip it in the fish sauce.  
**Manager:** There are a lot of herbs.  
**You:** Yes. They make the dish feel lighter.  
**Manager:** What’s inside?  
**You:** This one has shrimp and pork, but there are many other versions too.

## 3. Phở

Từ vựng: beef broth, chicken broth, rice noodles, rare beef, brisket, beef balls, bean sprouts, lime, chili, herbs.

Câu:
- The broth is the most important part.
- It takes a long time to prepare.
- You can add lime, chili, and herbs.
- Some people like rare beef.
- Others prefer well-cooked beef.
- It’s light but still filling.
- Many people eat pho for breakfast.

## 4. Bún chả / grilled pork noodles

Từ vựng: grilled pork, rice noodles, charcoal-grilled, smoky flavor, pickled vegetables, dipping sauce, fresh herbs.

- The pork is grilled over charcoal.
- It has a smoky flavor.
- You eat it with noodles, herbs, and dipping sauce.
- The pickled vegetables make it fresher.

Câu hỏi nối sang Pháp:
- Do you have many grilled meat dishes in France?
- Is barbecue common in summer?

## 5. Gỏi cuốn

Từ vựng: fresh spring rolls, rice paper, shrimp, pork, vermicelli, herbs, peanut sauce, fish sauce.

- These are fresh spring rolls.
- They’re not fried.
- They’re quite light.
- They’re wrapped in rice paper.
- You can dip them in peanut sauce or fish sauce.

## 6. Cơm tấm

Từ vựng: broken rice, grilled pork chop, fried egg, shredded pork skin, pickled vegetables.

- This is broken rice.
- It usually comes with grilled pork and a fried egg.
- It’s a very common lunch dish here.
- It’s simple but very filling.

## 7. Bún bò Huế

Từ vựng: spicy beef noodle soup, lemongrass, chili, beef, pork, broth, herbs.

- It’s a spicy beef noodle soup from Hue.
- The broth has lemongrass and chili.
- It’s stronger and spicier than pho.
- If you like spicy food, you should try it.

## 8. Cao lầu / Mì Quảng

**Cao lầu:** thick noodles, pork, herbs, crispy crackers, local specialty.

> Cao lau is a specialty from Hoi An. The noodles are thick and chewy. It comes with pork, herbs, and crispy crackers.

**Mì Quảng:** turmeric noodles, shrimp, pork, peanuts, herbs, small amount of broth.

> Mi Quang is from central Vietnam. It has less broth than pho. It usually comes with shrimp, pork, herbs, and peanuts.

## 9. Lẩu

Từ vựng: hot pot, broth, seafood, beef, vegetables, mushrooms, cook at the table, share dishes.

- Hot pot is good for sharing.
- We cook everything at the table.
- You can add seafood, beef, vegetables, and mushrooms.
- It’s popular when we eat in a group.

Câu hỏi:
- Do you often share dishes in France?
- Or does everyone usually order their own plate?

## 10. Bánh mì – điểm giao thoa Việt–Pháp

Từ vựng: baguette, pork, pâté, pickled vegetables, cucumber, herbs, chili.

- Banh mi uses a baguette, but the filling is very Vietnamese.
- It usually has pork, pâté, pickled vegetables, cucumber, and herbs.
- It’s a good example of Vietnamese and French food culture coming together.
- The bread is French-inspired, but the flavors are very local.

Câu hỏi:
- Does this remind you of sandwiches in France?
- What kind of baguette sandwich is common in France?

## 11. Cà phê Việt Nam

Từ vựng: Vietnamese coffee, condensed milk, iced coffee, strong coffee, drip coffee, robusta.

- Vietnamese coffee is quite strong.
- This one has condensed milk.
- We often drink it with ice.
- The taste is stronger and sweeter than many European coffees.

Nối sang Pháp:
- How do you usually drink coffee in France?
- Espresso?
- Do you drink coffee after lunch?
- Is coffee after lunch common in France?

## 12. Trà đá

- iced tea
- unsweetened tea
- refreshing
- common with lunch

- We often have iced tea with lunch.
- It’s usually very light.
- It’s refreshing in hot weather.
- It’s very common in local restaurants.

## 13. Món chay Việt Nam

Từ vựng: vegetarian, vegan, tofu, mushrooms, vegetables, soy sauce, plant-based.

- Vietnamese vegetarian food has a lot of variety.
- Many dishes use tofu, mushrooms, and vegetables.
- Some vegetarian dishes are made to look like meat dishes.
- It can be very light and flavorful.

## 14. Hải sản Việt Nam

Từ vựng: squid, crab, shrimp, prawns, clams, oysters, scallops, grilled seafood, steamed seafood.

- Seafood is very popular in coastal cities.
- Grilled seafood is common in the evening.
- Some places let you choose the seafood before they cook it.

## 15. Món đặc trưng của Pháp

**Croissant:** buttery, flaky, pastry, breakfast.  
> A croissant is light, buttery, and flaky. Do French people really eat croissants every morning?

**Baguette:** crusty, soft inside, bakery, fresh bread.  
> Is it common to buy fresh baguettes every day?

**Cheese:** soft cheese, hard cheese, blue cheese, goat cheese, strong smell, mild flavor, aged cheese.  
Câu hỏi: What cheese would you recommend to a beginner? Which cheese has the strongest smell? Do you usually eat cheese before or after dessert?

**Ratatouille:** vegetables, zucchini, eggplant, tomato, herbs.  
**Coq au vin:** chicken, red wine, slow-cooked.  
**Boeuf bourguignon:** beef stew, red wine, slow-cooked, Burgundy.  
**Quiche:** savory tart, eggs, cream, cheese, bacon.  
**Crêpe:** thin pancake, sweet, savory, filling.

## 16. Điểm chung Việt–Pháp

- **Bánh mì ↔ baguette:** ảnh hưởng Pháp trong ẩm thực Việt Nam.
- **Bánh xèo ↔ crêpe:** cùng dạng pancake mỏng nhưng vị và texture khác.
- **Coffee culture:** Việt Nam phổ biến cà phê đá; Pháp phổ biến espresso.
- **Pastry/cake culture:** Việt Nam có nhiều bánh từ gạo; Pháp có nhiều pastry từ bơ và bột mì.

Câu:
- Bánh xèo looks a little like a crêpe, but the taste is very different.
- One is savory and crispy, while a French crêpe is usually softer.
- We both have a strong food culture, but the ingredients are very different.

## 18. Công thức nói về bất kỳ chuyến du lịch nào

Dùng 6 bước: **Where is it? → Why is it special? → What can you do there? → When is the best time to go? → What should you eat there? → What should you be careful about?**

> **[Place]** is in **[region]**.  
> It’s famous for **[scenery / history / food]**.  
> You can **[activity]** there.  
> The best time to go is **[season/month]**.  
> You should try **[local dish]**.  
> Just be careful about **[heat / rain / crowds / traffic]**.

## 19. Việt Nam theo vùng và mùa

### Hà Nội
- Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake, Temple of Literature, street food, coffee culture.
- Spring: pleasant, sometimes humid.
- Summer: hot and humid.
- Autumn: often very pleasant.
- Winter: cool, sometimes cold.

Câu:
- Autumn is a very nice time to visit Hanoi.
- The weather is cooler and more comfortable.
- The Old Quarter is good for walking and street food.

### Hạ Long Bay
- limestone islands, cruises, caves, sea views.
- Ha Long Bay is famous for limestone islands.
- Many people take an overnight cruise.

### Ninh Bình
- limestone mountains, rivers, boat trips, rice fields.
- Ninh Binh is sometimes called “Ha Long Bay on land.”
- A boat trip is one of the best ways to see the area.

### Sapa
- mountains, rice terraces, trekking, ethnic minority villages.
- Cooler than lowland cities; can be foggy.
- Sapa is much cooler than Ho Chi Minh City.
- It’s good for trekking and mountain views.

### Huế
- Imperial City, royal cuisine, history.
- Hue is a good place if you like history.
- The food is also very different from southern Vietnamese food.

### Đà Nẵng
- beaches, seafood, Marble Mountains, easy access to Hoi An.
- Da Nang is a good mix of city and beach.
- It’s also close to Hoi An.

### Hội An
- ancient town, lanterns, food, riverside.
- Hoi An is beautiful in the evening.
- Cao lau is one of the local specialties.

### Nha Trang
- beach, seafood, islands.
- Nha Trang is more focused on beaches and sea activities.

### Đà Lạt
- cool weather, coffee, flowers, hills, pine forests.
- Da Lat is much cooler than Ho Chi Minh City.
- It’s popular for coffee, flowers, and relaxing weekends.

### Ho Chi Minh City
- Ben Thanh Market, Nguyen Hue Walking Street, Central Post Office, Notre-Dame Cathedral area, food, nightlife.
- Ho Chi Minh City is very lively.
- Evening is better for walking because it’s cooler.

### Mekong Delta
- rivers, fruit, boats, local life.
- The Mekong Delta is very different from the big cities.
- You can see more rural life and river culture.

### Phú Quốc
- beaches, seafood, resorts, sunsets.
- Phu Quoc is good for a relaxing beach holiday.
- Seafood and sunsets are two of the main highlights.

## 20. Nói về thời tiết và mùa ở Việt Nam

- The weather depends a lot on the region.
- Northern Vietnam has four clearer seasons.
- Southern Vietnam is more about dry season and rainy season.
- Central Vietnam can get heavy rain later in the year.
- You should check the weather before going to the coast.
- In hot weather, early morning and evening are better for walking.

Công thức:
> It’s usually **[hot / cool / rainy / humid]** at that time of year.  
> If you go in **[month/season]**, you may get **[rain / cooler weather / crowds]**.  
> I would recommend **[morning/evening]** for outdoor activities.

- If you like mountains, Sapa is worth visiting.

## 30. Mẫu hội thoại hoàn chỉnh

### A. Bánh xèo
**You:** Have you tried bánh xèo before?  
**Manager:** No. What is it?  
**You:** It’s a crispy Vietnamese pancake.  
**Manager:** What’s inside?  
**You:** Usually shrimp, pork, and bean sprouts.  
**Manager:** How do I eat it?  
**You:** Wrap a small piece with herbs and lettuce, then dip it in the fish sauce.  
**Manager:** Nice.  
**You:** The herbs make it feel much lighter.

### B. Phở
**Manager:** Is pho always beef?  
**You:** No, there’s chicken pho too.  
**Manager:** Which one do you prefer?  
**You:** Beef. I like the broth more.  
**Manager:** Is it spicy?  
**You:** Not by default. You add chili yourself.

### C. French cheese
**You:** What cheese would you recommend if I visit France?  
**Colleague:** Maybe Comté to start.  
**You:** Is it strong?  
**Colleague:** Not too strong.  
**You:** Good. I should probably not start with the strongest one.  
**Colleague:** Definitely not.  
**You:** Which one has the strongest smell?

### D. Vietnam travel
**Manager:** If I have three free days, where should I go?  
**You:** It depends on what you like.  
**Manager:** Maybe beach and food.  
**You:** Then Da Nang is a good choice. You can also visit Hoi An.  
**Manager:** Is it far?  
**You:** No, they’re quite close.

### E. France travel
**You:** If I visit France for the first time, where should I go besides Paris?  
**Colleague:** Maybe Lyon or Bordeaux.  
**You:** Which one is better for food?  
**Colleague:** Lyon.  
**You:** Good to know. What should I eat there?

## 31. Cách luyện

Mỗi ngày chọn:
- 1 món Việt
- 1 món Pháp
- 1 địa điểm
- 5 câu hỏi follow-up

Luyện theo cách:
1. Nói 30 giây về món ăn.
2. Tự hỏi 3 câu.
3. Tự trả lời.
4. Nói lại không nhìn tài liệu.
5. Hôm sau đổi chủ đề.

Mục tiêu: **không dịch từng câu từ tiếng Việt trong đầu, mà học theo cụm.**
