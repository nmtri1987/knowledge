# Travel vocabulary

## Planning and transport

| Collocation | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| travel plans | kế hoạch du lịch | Do you have any travel plans? |
| a short break | kỳ nghỉ ngắn | We are taking a short break in May. |
| an overnight trip | chuyến đi qua đêm | It is better as an overnight trip. |
| business trip | chuyến công tác | How was your business trip? |
| direct flight | chuyến bay thẳng | Is there a direct flight? |
| connecting flight | chuyến bay nối chuyến | I have a connecting flight in Singapore. |
| departure time | giờ khởi hành | What is your departure time? |
| travel time | thời gian di chuyển | The travel time is about two hours. |
| get around | di chuyển tại điểm đến | It is easy to get around by metro. |
| avoid rush hour | tránh giờ cao điểm | Try to avoid rush hour. |
| book in advance | đặt trước | You should book in advance during summer. |
| arrange transport | sắp xếp xe | Has the hotel arranged transport? |

## Experience and recommendations

| Collocation | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| worth a visit | đáng ghé thăm | The old town is worth a visit. |
| less crowded | ít đông | It is less crowded in the morning. |
| off the usual route | ngoài tuyến du lịch phổ biến | This village is off the usual route. |
| local experience | trải nghiệm địa phương | The market is a good local experience. |
| first-time visitor | người đến lần đầu | It is easy for a first-time visitor. |
| beautiful scenery | phong cảnh đẹp | The area has beautiful scenery. |
| travel light | mang ít hành lý | I try to travel light. |
| pack for the weather | chuẩn bị đồ theo thời tiết | Pack for hot and humid weather. |
| recover from jet lag | hồi phục sau lệch múi giờ | It took me two days to recover from jet lag. |
| look forward to | mong chờ | Which part are you looking forward to most? |

## Reusable patterns

- If you have **[time]**, I would suggest **[place]** because **[reason]**.
- The easiest way to get there is **[transport]**.
- It takes about **[duration]**, depending on traffic.
- For a first visit, I would spend **[number]** days there.
