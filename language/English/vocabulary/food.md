# Food vocabulary

Học theo cụm hoàn chỉnh để có thể dùng ngay khi ăn trưa hoặc tiếp khách.

## Taste and texture

| Word / collocation | Easy meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| mild | vị nhẹ | The sauce is quite mild. |
| spicy | cay | Is this very spicy? |
| savoury | vị mặn/đậm, không ngọt | I prefer savoury food. |
| slightly sweet | hơi ngọt | The sauce is slightly sweet. |
| rich | vị đậm hoặc béo | The soup is rich but not too heavy. |
| light | nhẹ bụng | I feel like something light today. |
| crispy | giòn | It is crispy on the outside. |
| tender | mềm, dễ ăn | The beef is very tender. |
| fresh | tươi | The herbs taste very fresh. |
| filling | ăn no | This dish is simple but filling. |

## Ordering and preferences

| Collocation | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a local specialty | đặc sản | This is a local specialty from central Vietnam. |
| the signature dish | món nổi bật của quán | What is the signature dish here? |
| dietary preference | lựa chọn ăn uống | Do you have any dietary preferences? |
| food allergy | dị ứng thực phẩm | Does anyone have a food allergy? |
| vegetarian option | món chay | Do they have a vegetarian option? |
| without peanuts | không có đậu phộng | Could we have this without peanuts? |
| on the side | để riêng bên cạnh | Could I have the sauce on the side? |
| share a few dishes | gọi vài món dùng chung | Shall we share a few dishes? |
| order for the table | gọi cho cả bàn | I can order for the table if you like. |
| split the bill | chia hóa đơn | Shall we split the bill? |

## Reusable patterns

- It is **[texture]**, a little **[taste]**, and it comes with **[ingredient]**.
- If you like **[type of food]**, I would recommend **[dish]**.
- It normally contains **[ingredient]**, but we can ask for it without that.
- I have not tried it, but it is one of the most popular dishes here.

## Mini dialogue

**Colleague:** What is this dish like?  
**You:** It is crispy, slightly sweet and served with fresh herbs.  
**Colleague:** Is it spicy?  
**You:** The sauce can be spicy, so ask for it on the side.
