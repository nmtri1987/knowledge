# Meetings

Dùng để mở cuộc họp, giữ đúng phạm vi, làm rõ quyết định và chốt action.

## Reusable phrases

| Situation | Natural sentence | Simpler alternative | More professional alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open | Let us start with the main decision. | What do we need to decide? | I suggest we begin with the decision we need today. |
| Clarify | Let me check that I understood you. | Do you mean this? | Let me confirm my understanding before we continue. |
| Refocus | Can we come back to the main point? | Back to the main point. | Could we return to the main question and park the detail for later? |
| Close | Let us confirm the owner and due date. | Who will do it, and when? | Before we close, could we confirm the action, owner, and target date? |

## Realistic dialogue

### A decision meeting

**PMO:** We have twenty minutes. Can we agree on an option today?  
**You:** Yes. I see two workable options. The first is safer for this release.  
**Architect:** Why do you prefer it?  
**You:** It changes less and gives us a clear rollback. The cost is one extra week.  
**PMO:** Can the team accept that?  
**You:** Let us confirm with the team now, then record the decision and owner.  

### When people speak fast

**Manager:** We should align the rollout with the platform window and the regional freeze.  
**You:** I caught the rollout part, but not the date. Could you repeat the date slowly?  
**Manager:** Thursday the twenty-sixth.  
**You:** Thanks. I will put Thursday the twenty-sixth in the chat so we all have the same date.  

## Useful vocabulary and collocations

- **decision required** — quyết định cần đưa ra
- **park this topic** — tạm để chủ đề sang phần sau
- **action owner** — người chịu trách nhiệm action
- **target date** — ngày dự kiến hoàn thành
