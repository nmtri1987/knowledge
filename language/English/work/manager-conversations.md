# Manager conversations

Cập nhật ngắn, chủ động, không phóng đại tiến độ và cho thấy mình đang kết nối các team.

## Reusable phrases

| Situation | Natural sentence | Simpler alternative | More professional alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Update | Quick update from my side: the review is on track. | The review is going well. | A brief update from my side: the review is on track, with one open dependency. |
| Blocker | We need input from the network team. | The network team needs to help. | The remaining blocker is the network decision; I have asked the owner to confirm by tomorrow. |
| Ask priority | Which item should I review first? | What is the top priority? | Could you confirm which item has the highest business priority this week? |
| Own follow-up | I will coordinate the next step. | I will follow up. | I will coordinate the technical follow-up and share a clear recommendation. |

## Realistic dialogue

### Weekly one-to-one

**Manager:** What did you focus on this week?  
**You:** I reviewed the GCP migration and helped two teams with production issues.  
**Manager:** What is the main risk?  
**You:** The migration test does not cover duplicate processing yet. I asked the team to add that case.  
**Manager:** Do you need support?  
**You:** Please help confirm the business owner for the acceptance criteria. I will handle the technical review.  

### When the answer is not ready

**Manager:** Can we approve the design today?  
**You:** I am not ready to recommend approval yet.  
**Manager:** What is missing?  
**You:** We need one load test and a rollback check. I can give you a clear answer by Friday morning.  

## Useful vocabulary and collocations

- **quick update** — cập nhật nhanh
- **open dependency** — dependency chưa được giải quyết
- **business priority** — ưu tiên nghiệp vụ
- **coordinate the follow-up** — điều phối bước tiếp theo
