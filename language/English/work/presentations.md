# Presentations

Cấu trúc phần trình bày ngắn cho architecture review, steering meeting và demo.

## Reusable phrases

| Situation | Natural sentence | Simpler alternative | More professional alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start | Today I will cover three points. | I have three points. | Today I will cover the current issue, the options, and my recommendation. |
| Signpost | The key point on this slide is the risk. | This slide shows the risk. | The main point to take from this slide is the operational risk. |
| Handle question | That is a good question. Let me answer it in two parts. | Let me answer in two parts. | Thank you. I would separate the technical answer from the delivery impact. |
| End | My recommendation is to start small and measure. | Let us start small. | My recommendation is a controlled pilot with clear success and rollback criteria. |

## Realistic dialogue

### Steering update

**You:** Today I will cover the issue, the options, and my recommendation.  
**Leader:** Can you start with the business impact?  
**You:** Of course. Five projects are affected, but production is still stable.  
**Leader:** What do you need from us?  
**You:** We need a decision on the pilot scope and one business owner for acceptance.  

## Useful vocabulary and collocations

- **key takeaway** — ý chính cần nhớ
- **business impact** — ảnh hưởng nghiệp vụ
- **controlled pilot** — pilot có giới hạn và kiểm soát
- **success criteria** — tiêu chí thành công
