# Technical Advisor - English

Mẫu câu giúp người khác thấy rõ bạn tư vấn, đánh giá, kết nối và hỗ trợ quyết định xuyên dự án.

## Reusable phrases

| Situation | Natural sentence | Simpler alternative | More professional alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Describe role | I help teams make sound technical decisions. | I help teams choose good solutions. | I advise teams on architecture, delivery risk, and production readiness across projects. |
| AI use | I use AI to explore options and speed up analysis. | AI helps me review and check ideas. | I use AI as a technical thought partner, then validate the output with project context and evidence. |
| Review | My role is to ask the questions that reduce risk. | I help find risks early. | I provide an independent technical view and help teams address risks before production. |
| Support | I do not take over the team’s work; I help them move forward. | I guide the team. | I support the team with options, trade-offs, and a clear path to a decision. |

## Realistic dialogue

### Explaining the TA role

**PMO:** Are you the technical lead for this project?  
**You:** The project has its own technical lead. I support several projects as a Technical Advisor.  
**PMO:** What does that include?  
**You:** I review important designs, highlight cross-project risks, and help teams prepare for production.  
**PMO:** Who makes the final decision?  
**You:** The accountable project owner makes it. I provide the technical recommendation and evidence.  

### AI coding survey

**Manager:** How are you using AI coding tools today?  
**You:** I use them to analyse issues, compare solution options, and understand unfamiliar code.  
**Manager:** Do you accept the first answer?  
**You:** No. I add business and technical context, then validate the result with tests and project evidence.  
**Manager:** What would improve the outcome?  
**You:** Better project context, clear security rules, and agreed quality checks.  

### Cross-project alignment

**Architect:** Our project needs a custom framework.  
**You:** What need is not covered by the common approach?  
**Architect:** We need a different approval flow.  
**You:** That sounds like a valid extension point. Let us keep the common core and isolate the approval rule.  
**Architect:** Can you help us review the boundary?  
**You:** Yes. I will compare it with the other projects and propose a reusable option.  

## Useful vocabulary and collocations

- **technical recommendation** — đề xuất kỹ thuật có lý do
- **independent view** — góc nhìn độc lập
- **cross-project risk** — rủi ro ảnh hưởng nhiều dự án
- **production readiness** — mức sẵn sàng production
- **thought partner** — đối tác cùng phân tích và phản biện
