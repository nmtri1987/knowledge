# Reusable speaking patterns

## Give a view without sounding absolute

- Natural: `From my perspective, the main risk is [risk].`
- Simple: `I see one risk: [risk].`
- Professional: `Based on the evidence we have, my main concern is [risk].`

## Move from problem to decision

- `The issue is [issue].`
- `It affects [flow/site/team].`
- `We have confirmed [fact].`
- `We still need to check [unknown].`
- `My recommendation is [option].`
- `We can validate it by [test/metric].`

## Ask for time when you do not know

- `I do not have enough context yet.`
- `I will check with the team.`
- `I will come back with a clear answer by [time + time zone].`

## Professional disagreement

- `I see the benefit. I also see one risk.`
- `Could we compare this with a simpler option?`
- `I am not comfortable approving this until we test the rollback.`

## European-style clear follow-up

> Thanks for the discussion. We agreed to [decision]. [Name] will [action] by [date]. The open point is [question]. I will follow up on [date].
